## 1. Verdict

M2.1 implements complete recent-draft precedence, one-time ordinary “海棠仙” seeding, local content-template management, and adding/removing one to twelve positive items. “海棠仙” has no built-in/read-only privilege and may be replaced, renamed, or deleted. Its independent seed marker survives deletion; automation proves that deleting the template, removing the recent draft, and reopening produces a blank Simple draft instead of resurrecting “海棠仙.”

This evidence comes from unit tests and desktop Chromium acceptance of the final `file://` artifact. It does not claim real Xiaohongshu Android/iOS host acceptance for cache lifetime, IME behavior, background restore, or system cleanup.

## 2. Implemented scope

- Added a stable, cover-free “海棠仙” seed fixture with fixed 8/7/9 whole-number scores, LV3, three reasons, work metadata, story, and an 80.0 aggregate assertion.
- Recent draft, template catalog, and one-time seed marker use three separate keys and validators. A valid recent draft always wins.
- The seed marker is written only after the catalog write succeeds; stable template ID prevents duplication on initialization retry. Seeded/no-draft startup creates a blank Simple draft.
- Content templates persist work, integer scores, reasons, deductions, and narrative while always clearing cover to `null`. The cover Blob remains memory-only.
- Added create, confirmed same-name replacement, confirmed application, rename, and confirmed deletion; names are 1–40 characters, catalog count is at most 20, serialized size is at most 512 KiB, and entries sort by update time.
- Added a best-effort synchronous recent-draft save on page hide/close while retaining the 300 ms debounce.
- Both locales explain that local cache is not permanent. At 360 CSS px, actions remain at least 44 px high with no horizontal scroll.
- Positive items support add, rename, whole-number scoring, and removal within a 1–12 item range; add/remove enters Custom mode and unrated items remain excluded from the aggregate.
- User-facing headings, buttons, help, status, and accessible names use neutral terms such as positive item, score, deduction item, and deduct, without describing icon shapes.
- The generation section and PNG explicitly identify the current output as a non-final chain-test image. The formal eleven-ratio card remains M3 work and is not claimed complete here.
