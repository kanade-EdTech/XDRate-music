# Decisions and Open Questions

This document separates defaults adopted to make development actionable from items requiring final product-owner confirmation. Defaults may change through an ADR but must not be silently contradicted in code.

## 3. Deferred items

### Politics-related ratings and passwords

The concept includes politics-related ranges, a blocked maximum score, password unlock, and reminders. Their goal, threat model, and legal/usability basis are not defined, and a client-side password cannot be a reliable security boundary. Therefore:

- v0.1 contains no politics preset, political media, or password feature;
- Any future version must first define the protected subject, password owner, recovery, bypass risk, scope, and legal/platform requirements;
- If the goal is preventing accidental action, prefer explicit confirmation and content warnings rather than claiming a frontend password is security.

### Anime and games

Anime, books, film/television, games, and other `XDRate {Domain}` areas remain future products or modes. Research their axes, scales, deduction rules, and media-rights strategy before connecting them to the generic engine; D-032 governs the initial candidates and their relationship to 1.0.0.

## 4. License and disclaimer baseline

- Source code uses the repository-root MIT License.
- The app provides a short privacy notice and a link to the complete disclaimer.
- The complete disclaimer explains that the tool runs offline and does not collect, store on a server, review, or transmit input; users are responsible for entered, generated, uploaded, and shared content, views, and copyrighted media; software is provided under the MIT “as is” terms.
- Do not promise that the browser or operating system leaves no local traces; promise only that the app does not intentionally upload data.
- A disclaimer cannot override applicable law. Obtain appropriate jurisdiction-specific review before public release.

## 5. Change process

When resolving a Q item:

1. Add a dated ADR or record the decision and rationale here.
2. Update PRD, functional specification, data model, and acceptance cases.
3. If old archives or scoring change, increment the relevant version and provide migration.
4. Synchronize `doc_CN` and `doc_EN` to prevent specification drift.
