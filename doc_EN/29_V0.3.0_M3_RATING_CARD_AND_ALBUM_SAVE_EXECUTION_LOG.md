## 1. Delivered

- The MiniTool Canvas renderer now consumes the production `MusicRatingDraft`, `calculateRating`, and the desktop eleven-ratio configuration instead of drawing a score-only probe image.
- All ratios are selectable: `16:9, 8:5, 3:2, 4:3, 5:4, 1:1, 4:5, 3:4, 2:3, 5:8, 9:16`. Changing the ratio invalidates the previous preview.
- Preview and album save call the same `createRatingCardPng` function; Save reuses the current preview PNG so it cannot silently render a different image.
- The card contains work metadata, the one-decimal 0–100 aggregate, mode, radar chart, independent score legend, axis reasons, story, and algorithm footer. Unrated axes remain excluded from the aggregate.
- The bridge remains strictly `writeTempFile` → `saveImageToPhotosAlbum`; cancellation or failure retains the draft and a later retry is allowed.
- Pixel-level rechecks on 2026-09-06 fixed score/`/100` overlap in narrow columns, 16:9 mode/story overflow, and excessive landscape-reason truncation. Landscape reasons now use three columns while square and portrait cards remain single-column.
- Radar and legend content now consume rated axes only. Every rated legend entry, the overall comment, and the personal story enter the PNG; a newly added unrated axis cannot pollute the result.
- The eleven user-supplied Haitang Xian samples exposed overlapping reason text at 4:3, needlessly stretched reason panels in portrait ratios, and inefficient landscape legend spacing. The renderer now sizes the reason panel from its content, selects one to three columns from the available space, and uses up to three legend columns in landscape cards. All eleven regenerated samples are free of text intersection and retain complete legend names.
